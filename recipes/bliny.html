Blinchiki - a piece of the sun



Ingredients

  • Milk (3% fat or more) 500ml, room temperature
  • Eggs: 2 (medium size), room temperature
  • Sugar: 2 table spoons
  • Salt: pinch or two
  • Wheat flour: 200gr (all purpose is good for this purpose)
  • Vegetable oil: 50ml (Sunflower is best, canola is a fraud)

The Mixture

  • Beat the eggs with the sugar until it looks uniform in color.
  • Add milk and mix until the mixture is uniform.
  • Add the salt
  • Add the flour, mix it in. Use a stick blender to crush all the flour spots until smooth.
  • Let the mixture sit for 15min
  • Give it a taste. You should taste a bit of salt and not just sweetness. Add a pinch of salt and mix if needed.
  • Add the vegetable oil and mix

Cooking

Any flat pan will do. 22cm low border flat is best imho (a creppe pan).

  • Heat the pan on a medium heat with a splash of oil. Remove the oil from the pan when hot.
  • Take the pan of the heat and pour a portion while moving the pan to make the mix cover the pan in a thin layer.
  • Pro tip: Pour a ladel of mix into the pan, swirl it around and whatever didn't stick to the pan - pour it out. Thats how you get the thinnest of blinchiki.
  • Pro tip: You should see small bubbles for on the pancake, if your pancake has no bubbles and reminds a piece of cloth in texture - add 50ml more vegetable oil and mix.
  • Train well to flip the blinchiki as a pro
  • A side is ready when it is a bit tan, no need to make it brown
